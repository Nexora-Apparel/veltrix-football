Only cache records whose `source_provider` is `OpenLigaDB` are attributed to this source. Those records also carry `source_license`, `source_license_url`, `source_attribution`, `source_url`, and `source_updated_at` fields. Other records retain their own source and licence metadata.

OpenLigaDB is a partial supplement for selected competitions. It is not treated as a complete inventory of all VELTRIX competitions and does not, by itself, clear a historical date gap. Unresolved dates remain explicitly listed in the cache state until the primary provider or a source with sufficient competition coverage verifies completeness.
