# StrikerAPI Data Governance and Model-Use Constraints

Status: provider documentation reviewed; contractual/legal sign-off remains a separate release gate.

Official references:
- https://strikerapi.com/docs/timing-and-rights
- https://strikerapi.com/docs/atlas
- https://strikerapi.com/pricing
- https://strikerapi.com/support

## 1. Permitted use (per current published provider summary)

StrikerAPI's published terms state that an account holder may:
- store data retrieved while the account is open;
- derive values from that data and use it for research, backtesting and training its own models;
- build commercial apps, websites and tools;
- display derived statistics, forecasts and individual data points, including individual odds, in its own product.

The published restrictions state that an account holder may not:
- redistribute the provider's data as a dataset, feed, file or separate API;
- redistribute raw or bulk odds/lines/prices as a feed or export;
- build a service competing with StrikerAPI using its data;
- represent the data as official league data or imply league/team/player endorsement.

The provider states that stored data may remain available for internal research/backtesting after cancellation, but continued user-facing product service based on the data requires an open account. These are summaries of the provider's published terms, not legal advice. Re-check the live terms before commercial release and retain a dated copy of the terms applicable to the account.

## 2. Attribution and league rights

The provider says attribution is not required, but it is independent and does not grant league, club or federation rights. Product branding, marks, logos and official reports require their own rights review. Do not imply official affiliation or endorsement.

## 3. Freshness and revision constraints

- Most non-odds datasets are refreshed daily, with the provider's published timing page specifying a daily refresh around 10:00 UTC for events and box scores; the general product page describes the daily refresh around 06:00 Eastern. Record the actual retrieval timestamp and verify the relevant endpoint's current freshness before use.
- Odds are refreshed hourly for matches in the next four days, according to the provider's published timing page.
- Event and box-score records may be refetched for roughly a day and a half after a match ends.
- The provider states that it keeps the current version of each record, not a per-record revision history. Corrections can overwrite a prior score, statistic or event.
- Therefore, every VELTRIX ingestion observation must retain retrieval timestamp, endpoint, source ID and canonical payload hash. Do not treat a later provider response as proof of what the provider showed at an earlier decision time.

## 4. xG and leakage controls

StrikerAPI's xG is generated by its own location model. The provider states that the model is fitted on each league's shots, including later seasons, and can be refitted, revaluing historical shots. Its xG is therefore not an as-of historical feature suitable for strict point-in-time backtesting by default.

Rules:
- Label it `provider_modelled_xg`; never label it observed xG.
- Do not use retrospectively refitted provider xG as a leakage-safe historical predictor in walk-forward validation.
- Prefer raw shot-location observations and fit a VELTRIX-owned xG model using only data available before each prediction timestamp.
- If provider xG is displayed for descriptive context, retain its provider/model provenance and retrieval timestamp and do not silently mix it with VELTRIX-owned xG.

## 5. Odds and value-betting constraints

- Odds are pre-match only; the provider says prices captured at or after kickoff are dropped.
- Opening means the first capture held by the provider, not necessarily the bookmaker's true first posting.
- Closing means the last capture before kickoff.
- Historical depth, bookmaker count and freshness vary by league and season. The atlas reports thinner coverage in recent daily feeds than in some historical periods.
- The provider's sportsbook odds archive does not include NWSL in the listed archive coverage.
- Do not infer 98% timestamped odds coverage from the presence of an odds endpoint. Measure per-fixture, per-market, per-book timestamp coverage and retain captured_at values.
- Provider odds alone do not establish value-betting certification; the VELTRIX prospective and walk-forward gates remain mandatory.

## 6. Competition-specific data quality

The atlas reports different history and completeness by competition and data type. Examples:
- Premier League shot locations are complete from 2013-14; 2012-13 is partial.
- Brazil and Argentina shot locations begin in 2014.
- NWSL shot locations begin in 2025; lineups are mostly available from 2019 and earlier periods are partial.
- Odds, lineups, events and box scores have separate coverage limits.

No dataset-wide completeness assumption is allowed. Maintain a coverage matrix keyed by competition, season, endpoint/data type and observed completeness.

## 7. Release gates

Before StrikerAPI data can feed a production model:
1. retain a dated copy of current provider terms and review commercial use;
2. verify account status and entitlement for every competition and endpoint;
3. establish freshness and data completeness by competition/season/data type;
4. preserve source observations and payload hashes;
5. prohibit retroactively revised provider xG from point-in-time backtests unless its historical as-of values can be independently demonstrated;
6. measure timestamped odds coverage empirically;
7. keep raw-feed redistribution disabled;
8. approve the source separately for each data type and competition.

This document does not authorize imports, paid upgrades, production model use or prediction delivery.

## 8. Dated operational terms and provenance review — 2026-11-11

Review basis: current public provider pages checked 2026-11-11. This is an operational product review, not legal advice or a signed licence.

### Account and access
- Free account: 500 credits each month; no payment card required.
- list_games costs 2 credits per request.
- Provider documents 60 requests/minute while credit balance is at least 100, and 10 requests/minute below 100.
- API key authentication supports x-api-key or bearer-header form. Store as GitHub Actions secret STRIKERAPI_API_KEY only.
- The 2026-11-09 bounded run used 29 calls / 58 credits. This proves the key worked and the four queried competition keys returned data at that time; it does not prove entitlement to all 17 competitions or every endpoint.

### Permitted use and restrictions
The current published timing-and-rights page permits storage while the account is open, deriving values, research, backtesting, training own models, and commercial display of derived statistics, forecasts and individual data points. It prohibits redistribution of the data as a dataset/feed/file/API, raw or bulk odds exports, competing data-feed products, and claims of official league/team/player endorsement. Stored data may remain for internal research after cancellation, but a user-facing product continuing to rely on the data requires an open account. No provider attribution is required. StrikerAPI does not grant league, club or federation rights.

Official terms: https://strikerapi.com/docs/timing-and-rights

### Provenance and source limitations
- Provider says it combines public sources and archives with its own models, but does not publish a source list per dataset.
- Provider xG and Elo are computed values, not raw observations. Label xG provider_modelled_xg, never observed xG.
- Fixtures/results are advertised from 2001-02 to current for matches listed; this is a coverage claim, not proof every competition/season has been independently reconciled.
- Shot-location coverage is competition-specific: top European leagues from 2012-13 (complete from 2013-14), Brazil and Argentina from 2014, NWSL from 2025. Lineups, box scores, events and odds have separate completeness limits.
- Odds are partial and bookmaker count varies. The sportsbook odds archive excludes NWSL. The provider does not supply an in-match live feed.
- Provider public product page says non-odds datasets refresh daily and odds refresh hourly for matches in the next four days. Record actual retrieval timestamps; do not infer historical as-of availability from today's response.
- Provider records can be corrected or overwritten; there is no per-record revision history. Retain endpoint, provider ID, retrieval time, payload hash and normalized value for each observation.

### Competition approvals from observed evidence
- EPL (soccer): fixture/results observed and independently cross-reconciled against 5Dollar for a bounded 90-day sample: 100/100 unique matches, zero conflicts, exact kickoff and score agreement.
- Argentina (argentina): bounded fixture/results probe returned 182 final and 8 scheduled rows; no second-provider overlap in the verified 5Dollar Free catalogue.
- Brazil (brazil): bounded fixture/results probe returned 124 final and 41 scheduled rows; no second-provider overlap in the verified 5Dollar Free catalogue.
- NWSL (nwsl): API probe returned 240 league-stage finals and 4 knockout-stage finals. Public NWSL coverage page says 224 for 2026. Official NWSL rules specify 16 teams with 30 regular-season games each (240 fixtures); official schedule footprint describes 248 matches including playoffs and Challenge Cup. The API's 240 league count agrees with the regular-season format, but the public-page count remains 16 lower. This is an evidence-based hypothesis, not a written provider explanation. Exclude NWSL from supported launch/import scope pending provider confirmation; do not ingest it or use it in production model features.
- Other 13 StrikerAPI competitions were not in the bounded four-competition qualification and are not approved by this record.

### Data-type release decision
- Fixture/results: approve StrikerAPI for EPL, Argentina and Brazil within observed entitlement and source-coverage limits. NWSL remains provisional.
- Lineups, box scores, events, shot maps, xG, Elo and odds: not approved as production model features by this fixture/results qualification; separate endpoint, completeness, timestamp and point-in-time validation is required.
- Raw provider feed redistribution remains disabled.
