## Coverage and known data quality

The v1-scope coverage file contains 119 competition-season records:
- 26,575 matches played;
- 26,536 matches with event data;
- 39 match gaps in v1 scope;
- 58 matches with known data issues in v1 scope.

The full supporting files contain:
- 1,606 known gaps across the broader release;
- 69 data-issue records overall: 14 few-events, 43 incomplete-lineup and 12 score-differs-from-events records.

Coverage is strong for the big-five leagues and selected UEFA/international competitions, but it is not global. The coverage file must be used to qualify every competition and season separately. Matches with known issues must be explicitly excluded or handled; they cannot be silently treated as complete.

## Model-use implications

This dataset materially improves the available historical feature inventory:
- player minutes, starts, positions and substitutions;
- player xG, non-penalty xG, xA, shots, passing, duels, tackles, interceptions, clearances and other match actions;
- team xG, shots, possession-related actions, passing and defensive actions;
- shot-level locations and situation labels;
- formation and lineup context.

OpenPlay xG is generated by the maintainer's own model. VELTRIX must retain the exact `xg_model` identifier and must not mix it with another provider's xG as if the definitions were identical. The event dataset has known qualifier and coverage differences across competitions and seasons.

The release is a static snapshot through 2025/26. It is not a current 2026/27 player-statistics, injury or confirmed-lineup feed.
